# Product Brief — AnalystAI

What your product does

- AnalystAI is a lightweight data intelligence platform that ingests CSVs, automatically detects data issues, cleans data, generates recommended charts, and provides AI-driven insights and Q&A over the dataset.

What problem it solves

- Non-technical users and analysts spend a large portion of time on data cleaning, chart selection, and writing summaries. AnalystAI automates the discovery, cleaning, visualization, and narrative generation steps so users can focus on decisions.

Who it’s for

- Data analysts, business users, product managers, and small teams who need fast, explainable analysis of tabular data without setting up pipelines or writing code.

Why it matters

- Accelerates insight generation from messy CSVs, reduces time-to-insight, and standardizes reporting. Embedding an AI backend (OpenAI Codex) enables natural-language Q&A over the data, democratizing analysis across non-technical stakeholders.
